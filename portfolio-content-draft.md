### What changed and why

- The existing hero was approved unchanged because it communicates the breadth of the work clearly.
- The project heading is "What I've built." The descriptions are shorter, with meaningful technical details retained. Categories, titles, stacks, destinations, and link labels remain intact.
- "Projects" replaces "Outside the day job." AppPilot appears in both the Microsoft experience and project list, so an exclusively personal-project heading would introduce an ownership assumption.
- The user confirmed that AppPilot currently supports Android, iOS, and web. Its description, type, footer, and About reference reflect that coverage. Its existing mobile illustration remains an example workflow, not an exclusive platform claim.
- InsideCode's description retains local repository exploration and optional citation-checked AI explanations. It does not promise that every optional AI configuration keeps all repository content on the machine.
- The original About heading and signoff were retained. The refined body describes the breadth of the work without repeating the Experience metrics.
- The Microsoft bullets preserve "contributed" for shared services and the qualified attribution of mobile-user acquisition. They do not claim sole ownership of team outcomes.
- The Skills section adds TypeScript, FastAPI, Azure OpenAI, PostgreSQL, and SQLite from the supplied project stacks. It retains the other current skills and does not assign proficiency ratings. Other project-specific tools stay on their project cards rather than expanding the main skills list further.
- The user clarified that the first Leadership role led placement coordination and approved "Lead Coordinator." The other titles and affiliations remain unchanged. No team sizes, placement totals, mentoring outcomes, or event attendance figures were invented.
- Clear existing section headings, the contact invitation, and navigation labels are deliberately retained.
- The public phone link is not repeated in this document. The website's contact destinations are unchanged.
